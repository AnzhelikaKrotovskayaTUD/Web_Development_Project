About the World of Supernatural

 
 

Supernatural is a popular American television series that combines elements of mystery, drama, detective, comedy and horror. The show was running for 15 years. However, following the SPN's creator's (Eric Kripke) steps, the creator of this website and the passionate fan of the series favoured only the first 3 seasons. That is the reason why this website is dark, moody, and full of mysteries.

 

We hope you will love this series as much as we do!
