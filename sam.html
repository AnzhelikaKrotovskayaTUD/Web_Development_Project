Sam Winchester


Little Brother


Sam's character is largely similar to his father's, the same determination and intransigence, and because of this they often quarreled. Sam also has a lot of his paternal grandfather's traits, high intelligence and a desire to find out all the details of the case he is working on. For this, Dean often called his younger brother a nerd.
