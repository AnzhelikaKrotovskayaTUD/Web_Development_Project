Dean Winchester


Big Brother


Unlike Sam, Dean has no secrets from his brother and no desire to change his life - he just lives as he is given in this world. He had many short-term relationships with women, but a truly serious relationship began only with Lisa Braeden. Dean lived with Lisa and her son Ben for a year and considered them his family.
