Vampires



Vampires descended from the Alpha Vampire. These creatures are immortal, and there are even those who are over 500 years old.


Appearance

Vampires are almost indistinguishable from humans, but under their gums are long and sharp fangs.



Witches



The witches usually summon a demon who gives them power. They are likely doomed to hell after their deal with the demon is over, as some of the demons were actually witches in life, like Ruby.


Appearance

They look like regular humans, but are more beautiful.



Werewolves



Werewolves look like humans, and at first glance they are indistinguishable from each other, only with the help of a camera, through which you can see how the retina of their eyes shines. Nothing is known about when exactly the werewolf abilities manifest - for some it happens immediately, and they transform from birth, others encounter it later.


Appearance

They can look like any regular human, but do not take photos of them - they do not like it, as their true form shines through.
